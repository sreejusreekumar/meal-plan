Kerala Meal Plan

4 weeks · multi-fish, no mutton · fibre first · walk after meals

--:--

Pick the Monday you began Day 1. The page then opens on the right day each morning, and repeats from Day 1 after Day 28.

Every day

  1. Plate orderThoran or mezhukkupuratti first, then fish, chicken or egg, then 1 cup matta rice last.
  2. Walk after meals10–15 minutes of brisk walking right after lunch and dinner.
  3. Cooking fishNo deep-fried meen porichathu. Kudampuli curry, pollichathu, or a tawa roast with 1 tsp coconut oil.
  4. Skip completelyKappa, pazham pori, mutton, bakery snacks and sweetened tea.
